# About

Hi, I’m Konstantin - a power systems engineer with a background in
power system analysis, modeling, and simulation.

I hold a PhD in Electrical Power Engineering from NTNU, where I worked on
protection algorithms and fault studies in smart grids. My professional
experience includes development of simulation and analysis tools for modern
power systems, with focus on dynamics, stability, renewable integration,
energy storage, and power electronics.

Currently I work with development of power system simulation tools and
mathematical models for analysis of complex electrical systems.

My main technical interests include:
- power system dynamics and stability
- EMT and transient simulations
- renewable energy integration
- FACTS and power electronics
- modeling and control systems
- engineering software development

This blog is a personal technical laboratory where I document experiments,
simulation studies, engineering notes, and things I learn along the way.
